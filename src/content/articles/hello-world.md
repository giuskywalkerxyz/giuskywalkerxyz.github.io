This is my holocron — a place to keep the knowledge I pick up breaking into machines, so it doesn't rot in a `notes.md` somewhere.

## What lives here

- **Writeups** — full, end-to-end walkthroughs of HackTheBox machines I've owned. Not just "here's the flag" but the *why* behind each step in the chain: the recon that mattered, the dead ends, and the one detail that unlocked the box.
- **Articles** — security research, tooling, technique deep-dives, and the occasional war story from the day job.

## The one rule

I only publish writeups for **retired / out-of-season** machines. Active-season boxes stay sealed until they retire — redacting the flag isn't enough, because *the attack chain itself is the spoiler*. That's HTB's rule and it's a good one.

So if a box I've owned isn't here yet, it's probably still active. It'll show up the day it retires.

## Why Star Wars

Because the handle is `giuskywalker`, and because offensive security really is a dark-side discipline: you learn the attacker's techniques precisely so you can defend against them. Red team is just the Sith training saber.

Give in to the dark side of the Force.
